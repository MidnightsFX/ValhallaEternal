# Valhalla Eternal
What if killing the final biome boss was not the end of the game? Do you seek a challenge? Do you want to show off to your friends?

This mod is designed to provide a reason to continue playing after your first victory. Some would consider this a Newgame+.
For the best experiance consider using [World Advancement Progression](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/VentureValheim/World_Advancement_Progression/).

This mod is designed to be a net neutral or net negative power balance. That isn't to say it doesn't give you more power and can make your journey significantly easier.
But it also provides options to increase your difficulty as your progress.

Want to know what the mods about? Well, try it! But if you really need a summary ahead of time, its like the Boon system from Hades turned valheim into a little more of a rougelite.

[![discord logo](https://i.imgur.com/uE6umQE.png)](https://discord.gg/Dmr9PQTy9m) [![github logo](https://i.imgur.com/lvbP5OF.png)](https://github.com/MidnightsFX/ValhallaEternal)

## Features

- A deity system providing boons and prestige for tribute
	- _Boons are positive buffs granted by deities, some boons require others before becoming available_
- Oaths these are vows which you must endure and will increase the difficulty of the game
- Prestige
	- Level  | A display of how many times you have made significant sacrifices (NG+) and accepted additional challenges, it is displayed for you and to other players
	- Auras  | Continued devotion to deities can reward visual effects which can be enabled/disabled as you see fit, purely cosmetic.
	- Titles | Completing challenging feats reward titles, these can be displayed for others to see.

## Compatibility
Can you use this with Epicloot? Can you use this with Jewelcrafting? _**Absolutely**_! I can't promise there will be no bugs or conflicts, but I am always interested in addressing those.

### Suggested Mod Pairing
- [World Advancement Progression](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/VentureValheim/World_Advancement_Progression/) - Make the NG+ part less of a breeze
- [Deathlink](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/MidnightMods/Deathlink/) - Let each player choose their death risks and rewards
- [Impactful Skills](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/MidnightMods/ImpactfulSkills/) - Make leveling up your skills more rewarding by giving them all more useful features
- [Star Level Systems](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/MidnightMods/StarLevelSystem/) - Expand creature levels, configure everything creature and loot related

## Gefjun | Meadows
Goddess Of Plowing, Agriculture & Protection

![Gefjun](https://github.com/MidnightsFX/ValhallaEternal/blob/master/ValhallEternalUnity/Assets/Art/gefjun_nobackground.png?raw=true)

Gefjun ensures a bounty and protection for those who are willing to offer sufficient tribute.

  | Boon Name | Description |
  | ----------- | ----------- |
  | Bounty of the lands | Chance to gain additional yields when harvesting plants. Increases gains and chance as leveled. |
  | Basic Protection | Provides a small amount of armor at all times. |
  | Quality Nourishment | Food items provide you with a bonus to health, stamina, and eitr depending on their value. Increases in power as leveled. |
  | Fishing Prosperity | A chance to gain random other items when fishing, loot table, item value and amount increase as the power is leveled. |

  Planned, but not implemented (subject to change)
