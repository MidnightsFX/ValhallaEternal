**0.1.0**
 ---
 ```
 - Valhalla Eternal is now Aesir Lineage, a new mod that replaces it.
 - Each deity now has a star map: earn that deity's star points and spend them on linked stars instead of raising boons one at a time.
 - Each shrine gives a quest to slay its biome's boss for a star point, then a short daily blessing that helps in that biome, and has a button to reset its deity's star map.
 - Points spent on stars are locked until you reset that deity's star map at one of its shrines.
 - Taking a star plays a sound for its tier, the same for every deity.
 - Every power in the star map now explains how it works, both before and after you take its star.
 - Enemies finished off by burning or poison after your hit count as your kills, so kill powers trigger for them.
 - Powers that save you from death keep their cooldown when you die.
 - Retort, Arrow Catcher, Keep the Secret and the Inseparable oath now work as described, and star descriptions that misstated a power are corrected.
 - Deities sometimes reward kills of starred enemies, crafting and harvesting with a star point, up to a limit each run.
 - The first kill of each miniboss gives its deity a star point.
 - Every herald of Ragnarök slain in an Eternal Hall world gives its deity a star point.
 - A deity you are devoted to may send you a dream while you sleep, lending you a stronger boon for a while.
 - A boss leaves its Soul of Power whenever someone nearby hasn't claimed it yet, and each player can claim a star point from it once per run.
 - Every deity's star map is complete, with a path for each of the deity's aspects, bridges between the paths and a final star on each that opens with Ascension.
 - Each deity brings powers drawn from their myths, from crops that grow anywhere and falcon flight to rune domes, frost bursts and summoned allies.
 - Each deity's star map has more paths to choose from, covering taming, the home, crafting, sailing, spears, polearms, meads, trade, poison, summons and the night.
 - Small stars now fill the space between neighbouring paths, so a build can cross from one path to another in more places.
 - Adds Víðarr, the silent god of the Deep North, with his own shrines and star map, and the final boss leaves his Soul of Power.
 - The strongest stars come with oaths, drawbacks that last while you keep the star.
 - Each deity has its own look, from its Soul of Power to an aura, wings, footprints, a weapon glow and a kill effect, chosen in the star map's new Looks panel.
 - Powers flash in the colours of the deity they come from, and the bigger ones are seen by everyone nearby.
 - The star map glows, with light flowing through the stars you hold and runes circling each deity.
 - Devotions are now Ascension: start over in a deity's name to open their final stars, earn their points and keep their looks for good.
 - Ascending starts a new run: every Soul of Power can be claimed again, and quests, blessings and lucky rewards start over.
 - Looks take an ascended form that grows with each Ascension, and the star map shows how far you have ascended with each deity.
 - The old boons now do what they describe; several never worked and others were far stronger than intended.
 - Adds an optional progression lock that seals gear, food, crafting and building from each biome until you have defeated the boss before it.
 - Servers now share their shrine and star map settings with everyone who joins.
 - Other players' cosmetics are now shared safely.
 - A star map screen (press K, click the star at the end of the inventory's row of round buttons, or use any shrine) shows each deity's stars around their portrait and previews what each star would change.
 - With Auga, the star map's button is in the row under your character's name.
 - Works with the newest Auga, and keeps its own look there.
 - Fixes errors when opening the compendium with Auga.
 - Console commands can also list and spend star points, and help admins test quests, blessings, dreams and lucky rewards.
 - Admins can give star points to any online player or take them away.
 - Tab fills in each console command's deities, players and other choices.
 - A short setup tutorial opens on first launch, and any time after from Mod Config, to choose how star points are earned, whether deity looks are shown and what Ascension costs.
 - Servers can turn deity looks off for everyone.
 - Each Ascension brings Eternal Hall's Ragnarök closer for everyone, while always leaving a little warning.
 ```
